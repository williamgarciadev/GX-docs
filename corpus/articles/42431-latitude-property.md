# Latitude property

Indicates a specific latitude to center the map.

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Generators:** [.NET](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?38604), [.NET Framework](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?2892), [Java](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?12258)  
**Controls:** [Map](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5029)

### [Description](#Description)

Consider you must enter a specific coordinate value.

### [Samples](#Samples)

To center the map in New York, you have to configure the properties as follows:

City = Specific coordinates  
Longitude = -74.04450042155374  
Latitude = 40.68942013686783
