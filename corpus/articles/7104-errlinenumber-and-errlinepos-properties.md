# ErrLineNumber and ErrLinePos Properties

Returning the line number and position within it respectively, when ErrCode is other than zero.

### [Syntax](#Syntax)

**&***DataType***.ErrLineNumber**  
**&***DataType***.ErrLinePos**  
  
**Type Returned:**   
Integer

### [Description](#Description)

Line number and position within it respectively are returned, when ErrCode is other than zero.

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Extended Data Types:** [XmlReader](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?6928)  
**Languages:** .NET, Java, Ruby (up to GeneXus X Evolution 3), Visual FoxPro (up to GeneXus X Evolution 3)
