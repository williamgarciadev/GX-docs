# Business Process Management (BPM)

Business Process Management (BPM) is a method focused on continuously managing and optimizing an organization’s activities and [business processes](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?48918), including management practices, policies, control processes and metrics, as well as software tools that support all such activities.
