# GeneXus 18 Samples

This page provides a list of samples prepared for GeneXus 18. They show different functionalities and features this GeneXus version has, hence they will help you get deep insights into what can be achieved in different areas of mission-critical applications development.

Each sample typically consists of a [KB](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?2428), Figma or Sketch design with the corresponding sample data, and also a step-by-step guide to successfully prototype it.

To facilitate access, some of these samples also appear in the [GeneXus Start Page](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?52662).
