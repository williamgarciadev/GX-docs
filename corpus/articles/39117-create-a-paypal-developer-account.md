# Create a Paypal developer account

To integrate with Paypal’s platform, you need to create a developers account.

Follow these steps:

1. Go to [Paypal's developer site](https://developer.paypal.com)
2. Click on “Log into Dashboard”
3. Click on “Create account”
4. Select your type of account
5. Follow the steps and fill the forms with your information
6. Finally, confirm your account (from your e-mail)
