# Filters position property

Filters position in the dashboard layout.

### [Values](#Values)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Left** | Filters are shown in the left side of the screen. |
| **Right** | Filters are shown in the right side of the screen. |
| **Top** | Filters are shown at the top of the screen. |

### [Description](#Description)

All [Dashboard Filters](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?36769) are grouped together on the same region of the screen and can be shown at the right, left or top side of the screen.

### [Runtime/Design time](#Runtime%2FDesign+time)

This property applies only at design-time.
