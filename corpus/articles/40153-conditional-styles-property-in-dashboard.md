# Conditional styles property in Dashboard

Customizes the Data Axis output’s appearance depending on whether a certain condition is met.

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Objects:** Dashboard

### [Description](#Description)

Enables customizing the [Data Axis](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?40144) output’s appearance depending on whether a certain condition is met. Check [Styles and Conditional styles](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?23523,,) for further detail.

### [Runtime/Design time](#Runtime%2FDesign+time)

This property applies only at design-time.

### [Availability](#Availability)

This property is available since [GeneXus 15 upgrade 12](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?39737,,).
