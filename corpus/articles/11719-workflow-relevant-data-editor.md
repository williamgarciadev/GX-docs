# Workflow Relevant Data Editor

You may define global variables for a [Business Process Diagram object](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?16486).  
  
Business Process Diagrams have a selector that lets you open the [Relevant Data](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?11759) Editor. For example:

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 52109]`

Look at the Properties Editor on the right. There, you can set the different [properties](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?11748) related to the relevant data being defined or edited.
