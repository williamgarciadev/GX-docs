# Subject rule property

Allows defining which will be the subject of the Task at runtime.

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Controls:** [Task](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?17495)

### [Description](#Description)

You can use characters, [Attribute](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?6802) and [Relevant Data](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?11759) (eg."Customer registration number"+CustomerId+&relevantData).

### [Runtime/Design time](#Runtime%2FDesign+time)

This property applies only at design time.
