# DesignOps - Other samples

This article describes a set of design files made with the [Sketch design tool](https://www.sketch.com/) and exported by [GxDesignOps plugin](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?46875) in .gxsketch format.

**Warning**: Do not expect a perfect import. It is highly likely that you or your designer will have to fix something in the design file or the generated objects (abstract layout or theme-class).
