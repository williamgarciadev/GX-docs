# Master Panel property

Assigns one of the Master Panels in the Knowledge Base to a Panel object.

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Objects:** [Panel](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?24829)  
**Generators:** [Angular](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?42550)

### [Runtime/Design time](#Runtime%2FDesign+time)

This property applies only at design-time.

### [Availability](#Availability)

This property is available since [GeneXus 17](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?46066,,).
