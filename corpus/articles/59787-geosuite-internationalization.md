# GeoSuite - Internationalization

In the context of spatial data, Internationalization (I18n) is reflected, among other things, in the mapping providers used in different parts of the world.

For example, Baidu is a specific mapping provider in the Chinese market. In the Western world, Google and Apple are among the most popular in this field, however, there are other providers, such as Mapbox, Here, or Arcgis.

This section lists the mapping providers offered by GeneXus on its different platforms. They are horizontal providers, that is, their scope covers all the functionalities described in the marketecture.
