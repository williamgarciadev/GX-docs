# Constants

The language of GeneXus objects has mainly three kinds of constants:

* Literal constants
* Numeric constants
* [Date Constants](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?4372)

They can be used to make up terms of expressions, as right side of assignations (to variables or attributes), or sent as parameters to different objects.
