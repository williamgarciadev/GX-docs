# EventDay with Fiori Backend

This sample is the [EventDay](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?22550,,) sample with a backend using [GeneXus for SAP Systems - Fiori Mobile Pattern](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?39800)

It could be the app for the upcoming event or conference your company organizes, allowing your guests to get all the information about it.

* Schedule your favorite conferences
* See all the information about the speakers, rooms, sessions
* Check out what people are saying about the conference on twitter

It's an Omni-channel front-end: Web Responsive, Native Android and iOS applications with a neutral look & feel; and has a Web Responsive Fiori backend.

You can download this sample from GeneXus for SAP by going to the Start Page->Samples and selecting it.
