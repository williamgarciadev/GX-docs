# GXflow Task Performance

Task performance analysis in GXflow enables the evaluation of the status and timing management of tasks within business processes, providing valuable information for decision-making. This module includes the following indicators:

#### [Task Status General](#Task+Status+General)

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 59984]`  
Purpose:  
Visualize the percentage of active task instances classified as 'on time', 'at risk', or 'delayed'.  
Usefulness:  
Allows a quick assessment of the overall status of running tasks and the detection of potential deadline compliance risks.  
Interpretation:  
The chart displays a distribution of active tasks that helps identify whether there are accumulations in risk or delay status.

#### [Task Status Details](#Task+Status+Details)

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 59985]`  
Purpose:  
Break down the status of active instances by task type and time classification.  
Usefulness:  
Helps identify specific tasks with higher risks of non-compliance or delays, guiding targeted improvement actions.  
Interpretation:  
The bar chart allows comparisons across different task types to determine which require priority attention based on their risk or delay levels.

#### [Next Task Deadlines](#Next+Task+Deadlines)

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 52474]`  
Purpose:  
Show how many task instances are due in the coming days.  
Usefulness:  
Supports proactive deadline management by prioritizing tasks that are closest to their due date.  
Interpretation:  
The chart reveals possible clusters of upcoming deadlines, enabling the planning of preventive actions to avoid breaches.
