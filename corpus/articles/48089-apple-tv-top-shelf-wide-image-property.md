# Apple TV Top Shelf Wide Image property

Sets a static Apple TV Top Shelf Wide Image. It is displayed when your app is in the Dock and in focus.

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Objects:** [Menu](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?16321), [Panel](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?24829), [Work With](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?15974) (Only [Main Objects](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5770))  
**Generators:** [Apple](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?14917)

### [Description](#Description)

Indicates the image to use in the Apple TV Home screen when your app is selected in the Dock.

Apple's documentation describes the top shelf as follows:

> The Apple TV Home screen uses the top shelf to showcase your content in a rich, engaging way while also giving people access to their favorite apps in the Dock.

The image provided in this property should support @1x and @2x densities with the following dimensions:

* 2320x720 for @1x density, and
* 4640x1440 for @2x density.

Note that for narrower screens the [Apple TV Top Shelf Image property](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?48088) will be used instead.

### [Runtime/Design time](#Runtime%2FDesign+time)

This property applies only at design-time.
