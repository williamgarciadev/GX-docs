# GeneXus Editors

[GeneXus](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?1756) offers several editors to help you to declare, define, set, and design the different kinds of definitions in your [Knowledge Base](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?1836)s.

Below, there is a list of some editors:

* [Objects Editors](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?6641)
* [Structure Editor](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?3913)
* [Variables Editor](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?3173)
* [Properties Editor](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?3160)
* [Source Code Editor](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?3914)
* [Diagram Editor](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?23941)
* [Table Editor](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?3928)
