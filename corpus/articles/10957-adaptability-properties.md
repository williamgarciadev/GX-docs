# Adaptability Properties

This group of propeties helps you give greater flexibility to processes (in a [Business Process Diagram](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?16486)'s tasks), so that they adapt to different scenarios, including those that are less structured.

They can be found under the node **Advanced Properties> Adaptability:**

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 33043]`

Note that the last three properties are only available when the [Is ad-hoc property](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?11875)=True.
