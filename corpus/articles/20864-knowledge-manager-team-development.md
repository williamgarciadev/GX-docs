# Knowledge Manager Team Development

Knowledge Manager Team Development provides the mechanism for interacting with a [GeneXus Server](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?9911) instance. It is accessed through the [Knowledge Manager Menu](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5679) by selecting the *Team Development* option.
