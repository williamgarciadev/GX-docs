# Preferences (GeneXus 18 latest upgrade or prior)

The Preferences window allows you to configure the [Knowledge Base properties](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/com.gxwiki.wiki?33001,,) and [Version properties](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/com.gxwiki.wiki?7860,,), as well as manage [Environments](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?7115), [Generators](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?7116) and [Data Stores](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?7117).

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 54586]`

To open the Preferences window, go to **View > Other Tool Windows > Preferences**:
