# Alignment property

Determines the position of the text within the control.

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Controls:** [Text Block](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5948), Attribute/Variable

### [Description](#Description)

This property applies exclusively to [Text Block](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5948) and Attribute/Variable controls in the [Procedure's Layout](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5468). It allows you to choose the position of the text inside the control.

The possible values the property can take are TopLeft, TopCenter, TopRight, MiddleLeft, MiddleCenter, MiddleRight, BottomLeft, BottomCenter, BottomRight, TopJustify, MiddleJustify, and BottomJustify.

Selecting an option containing 'Justify' establishes the distribution of extra whitespace evenly throughout the rows of the paragraph. The text aligns with left and right indents, promoting a visually balanced presentation. Notably, while maintaining left-to-right alignment, the last line is specifically aligned to the left.

### [Runtime/Design time](#Runtime%2FDesign+time)

This property applies only at design time.
