# HowTo: Get the Consumer Key and Consumer Secret codes from Twitter

The purpose of this article is to explain the necessary steps to associate your application with Twitter by the *Consumer Key* and *Consumer Secret* certificates.

### [Step 1 - Sign in as developer](#Step+1+-+Sign+in+as+developer)

Go to [Twitter Developer website](https://apps.twitter.com/) and sign in. If you do not have an account, create it first.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 35888]`

### [Step 2 - Create a new app](#Step+2+-+Create+a+new+app)

Look for the *Create New App* button and click on it.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 35889]`

Then, give your application details

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 35892]`

Finally, confirm the agreements and click on *Create your Twitter application* button.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 35893]`.

Twitter should confirm that your application was successfully created.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 35894]`

### [Step 3 - Get the Consumer Key and Secret codes](#Step+3+-+Get+the+Consumer+Key+and+Secret+codes)

Go to the *Key and Access Tokens* tab, and you'll find the *Consumer Key* and *Consumer Secret* codes.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 35895]`
