# Form Definition

[GXflow Form Definitions](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?25811) are used to create forms that can change dynamically without the need for coding, compiling, generating and deploying your Workflow applications. These Form Definitions are then used from [User Tasks](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?17499)—[Application form property](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?25827,,)— and called upon their execution.  
See [GXflow Dynamic Forms](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?25809) for further details.
