# Corner Radius property

Indicates the radius for the Rectangle corners (in pixels).

### [Description](#Description)

The Corner Radius property is available for Rectangle controls (inserted within a [Printblock control](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?1958) in a [Procedure Layout](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5468)).
