# Domains with Special Semantics

Some standard domains have special semantics that produce special behavior on fields based on these domains when executed in Smart Devices.

For example, let's suppose that a field is based on the Address domain and that the field is shown in readonly mode on a Smart Device. The application, when the user clicks on a balloon shown beside the field, will redirect to a Google map showing that location.

Domains with special semantics are:

* [Address domain](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?14636)
* [Component domain](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?16186)
* [Email domain](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?14650)
* [Feed domain](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?16213)
* [Geolocation domain](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?14644)
* [HTML domain](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?16293)
* [Phone domain](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?14639)
* [URL domain](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?15668)
* [Time domain](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?15050)
