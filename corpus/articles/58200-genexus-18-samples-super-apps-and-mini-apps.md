# GeneXus 18 Samples: Super Apps and Mini Apps

This documentation showcases essential [Super Apps](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/com.gxwiki.wiki?50900,,) and [Mini Apps](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/com.gxwiki.wiki?50900,,) samples. These samples demonstrate the capabilities of GeneXus 18 for creating Super Apps and integrating Mini Apps.

### [GeneXus Super Apps](#GeneXus+Super+Apps)

* [Verdant Bank - GeneXus Super App Sample](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?56766)

The Verdant Bank App exemplifies the capabilities of a GeneXus Super App designed to replicate a comprehensive banking experience.

This Super App hosts three Mini Apps:

[The Movies](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?56785): This Mini App facilitates the purchase of movie tickets.  
[Frosty Delights](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?56786): Users can order a variety of ice cream flavors through this Mini App.  
[Coffee and Muffins](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?56784): This Mini App allows users to order coffee and sandwiches from a coffee store.

### [Non-GeneXus Super Apps](#Non-GeneXus+Super+Apps)

These samples show evidence that an application not developed with GeneXus technology can be converted into a Super App because GeneXus licenses the necessary technology to do so. Check the different languages and frameworks samples:

* [Kotlin (Android)](https://github.com/genexus-books/gx-super-app/tree/main/Android)
* [Swift (iOS)](http://ttps://github.com/genexus-books/gx-super-app/tree/main/iOS)
* [React Native](https://github.com/genexus-books/gx-super-app/tree/main/ReactNative)
* [Flutter](https://github.com/genexus-books/gx-super-app/tree/main/Flutter)

These Super Apps host this Mini App:

* [Mini App Payments - Mini App Sample for non-GeneXus Super App API integration](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?58157)

In this context, Mini App Payments stands out as an example of a Mini App designed to integrate into non-GeneXus Super Apps. This Mini Application demonstrates how payment functionalities can be easily incorporated into a Super App.

This sample illustrates how payment features are incorporated into the native framework of the Super App.
