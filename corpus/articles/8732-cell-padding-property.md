# Cell Padding property

You must enter the required distance between the contents and the cells' borders in pixels.

### [Syntax](#Syntax)

**control.** CellPadding = expression   

**Where:**  
*Control*  
    Is the name of a control inserted in the form.  
  
*expression*  
    It specifies the distance in pixels between the contents and the cells' borders.  
  
**Default Value**= 1

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Generators:** [.NET](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?38604), [Java](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?12258)  
**Controls:** [Free Style Grid](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?6058), [Grid](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?24817), [Table](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?6001)

### [Description](#Description)

**Design Time:**Applies to the Grids and Tables.  
**Execution Time:** Applies to the Free Style Grids, Grids, Tables.
