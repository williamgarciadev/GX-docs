# Hyperlink control

Hyperlink controls give the user the possibility of establishing links to pages other than the current one. They are usually used when the link is static.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 6008]`

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 6009]` To add a Hyperlink to the selected location, drag one from the toolbox Hyperlink icon and onto the webform. In the image you can see we inserted the following hyperlink: The Weather.

After you insert a hyperlink in the webform object, you can configure its properties by editing the control's properties window.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 6010]`

In our example, The Weather links to the weather forecast at [www.wunderground.com](http://www.wunderground.com). Once you have dragged & dropped the hyperlink into the webform, change the "Hyperlink" text to "The Weather" and write the URL in the URL property.
