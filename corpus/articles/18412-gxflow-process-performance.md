# GXflow Process Performance

GXflow process performance analysis provides a comprehensive view of the evolution and compliance of processes over time, facilitating the early identification of deviations and improvement opportunities.

#### [Process Activity Trend](#Process+Activity+Trend)

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 52465]`  
Purpose:  
Visualize the daily evolution of process instances that are opened, closed, aborted, and active.  
Usefulness:  
Allows the identification of trends, activity peaks, or recurring problems in process behavior over time.  
Interpretation:  
A general increase in opened and active instances is observed during the analyzed period, which may indicate a rise in workload or the need to strengthen operational resources.

#### [Process Status General](#Process+Status+General)

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 59982]`  
Purpose:  
Show the percentage of active instances that are on time, at risk, or delayed.  
Usefulness:  
Provides a quick overview of the overall health of active processes, helping detect potential SLA (Service Level Agreement) breaches.  
Interpretation:  
100% of active instances are on time, reflecting adequate control over ongoing processes.

#### [Process Status Details](#Process+Status+Details)

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 59983]`  
Purpose:  
Break down the status (on time, at risk, or delayed) of active instances by process type.  
Usefulness:  
Enables a detailed analysis of each process, supporting targeted corrective actions where necessary.  
Interpretation:  
One instance of the 'Purchases' process and two instances of the 'Ticket Reservation' process are at risk, suggesting the need for monitoring and immediate preventive actions.

#### [Next Process Deadlines](#Next+Process+Deadlines)

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 52467]`  
Purpose:  
Display upcoming dates by which process instances must meet their deadlines.  
Usefulness:  
Helps anticipate potential due dates, allowing efforts to be prioritized on instances approaching their deadline.  
Interpretation:  
One instance is scheduled to reach its deadline on 10/26/22, allowing for proactive management to ensure compliance.
