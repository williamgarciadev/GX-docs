# GXflow My Performance

The My Performance application allows the user to view the tasks he has completed and compare his performance with that of his peers.

The following figure shows My Performance interface:

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 53988]`

You can choose the task and the dates you want to compare with using the Process, Task, From, and To combo boxes.
