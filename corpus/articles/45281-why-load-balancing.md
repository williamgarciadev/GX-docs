# Why Load Balancing

Load Balancing is an important concept for the [Scalability and Performance of GeneXus Applications](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?45280).

It helps you get High Availability, Fault Tolerance and Update with Zero Downtime

Furthermore, it is used to maintain performance in horizontal scaling
