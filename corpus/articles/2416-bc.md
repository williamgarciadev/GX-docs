# BC

Acronym of [Business Component](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5846).

Business Components (BCs) provide a way to use all the power of transactions from other GeneXus objects, and to invoke them without the need for a user interface.

See [Business Component](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5846).
