# Statistics and business process optimization

This stage of the [BPM Cycle](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?44029) refers to the actions taken to improve the performance of a Business process modeled and executed with the GeneXus BPM Suite.

The GXFlow client provides us real-time statistics which give us the possibility to perform analysis of process, task and work teams.

The available statistics are:

* [GXflow Process Analysis](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?18410)
* [GXflow Task Analysis](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?18411)
* [GXflow Process Performance](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?18412)
* [GXflow Task Performance](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?18413)
* [GXflow Team Performance](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?18414)
