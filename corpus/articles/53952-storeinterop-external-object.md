# StoreInterop external object

The StoreInterop external object allows you to integrate and use Ratings and Reviews within your applications without having to leave them. In other words, you can ask your app users for feedback by selecting Rating stars and also by entering Reviews of them.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 53953]`

You can find the StoreInterop object in the [KB Explorer](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?3210) within the Store module, which is located within the SD module. That is to say, it is part of the [Smart Devices API](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?15288).

## [Properties](#Properties)

It doesn't have any.

## [Methods](#Methods)

### [AskForReview](#AskForReview)

Prompts the end user to leave a rating and comment on the application.

| --- | --- |

## [Requirements](#Requirements)

### [Android Requirements](#Android+Requirements)

* Android devices (phones and tablets) running Android 5.0 (API level 21) or higher that have the Google Play Store installed.
* Chrome OS devices that have the Google Play Store installed.
* Not Available for Huawei devices yet.

### [Apple Requirements](#Apple+Requirements)

* iOS 10.3 or higher

## [Availability](#Availability)

This external object is available since [GeneXus 18 Upgrade 5](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?54239)
