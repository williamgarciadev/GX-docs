# Ads (Advertising)

A marketing and promotion activity; draw attention to (generally to sell goods or services).

You can incorporate ads using an *[Ads View control](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?32653)* that you can drag from the toolbox to any section of the layout.
