# RTL

RTL or [Right to Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right-to-left) is a writing system where it starts from the right of the page and continues to the left. This can be contrasted against left-to-right writing systems, where writing starts from the left of the page and continues to the right. Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, and Urdu are some examples of RTL writing systems.
