# Create Stripe Account

To create a Stripe Account, you need to follow these steps:

1. Go to <https://stripe.com>

2. Create Account

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 37947]`

3. Fill the form

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 37948]`

4. An Email will be sent to your inbox. Activate your account with it.
