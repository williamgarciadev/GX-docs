# Background property

Selects the image for the panel's background.

### [Syntax](#Syntax)

**control.** Background = expression   

**Where:**  
*control*  
    Is the name of a control inserted in the form  
  
*expression*  
    It specifies the image's URL

### [Description](#Description)

The required value is the image's URL. You must use bitmaps with .JPG or .GIF extension. The navigator automatically repeats the image horizontally as well as vertically in order to fill in the entire window. We recommend that they be in a directory under the root one of the Web Server.  
  
**Design Time**: Applies to the forms, Tables, Table' Lines and Table' Cells.  
  
**Execution Time**: Applies to the forms, Free Style Grids, Grids and Tables.

### [Runtime/Design time](#Runtime%2FDesign+time)

This property applies both at run-time and at design-time.

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Objects:** Transaction, Web Panel  
**Platforms:** Web(.Net, Java)  
**Controls:** FreeStyle Grid, Grid, Table
