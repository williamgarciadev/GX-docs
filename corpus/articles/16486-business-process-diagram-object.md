# Business Process Diagram object

Represents the flow of a business process that occurs in real life. The process modeler used in the GeneXus IDE is the same as the one used in the [Business Process Modeler](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?20259). To model correctly it is necessary to have some knowledge about [BPMN](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?24913), in the GeneXus Wiki there are several articles about it.

A Business Process Diagram object has several sections that may be defined, for example:

* Diagram: It allows you to draw the business process. To do it, you can drag and drop the objects (such as web panels) or you can use the [Toolbox](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?16487).
* [Relevant Data](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?11759): It is used to mantain a global area of data in a process, this allows managing the transfer of information between tasks.

As most of the objects, it also has a section for [Documentation](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?6685) as well as a [Properties Editor](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?3160), to set up general aspects of the object.
