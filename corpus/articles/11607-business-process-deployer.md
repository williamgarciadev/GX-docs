# Business Process Deployer

This tool helps developers deploy their [Business Process Diagram object](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?16486) and, consequently, impact the production database. It is distributed with the GeneXus installation and located in <GeneXus Installation path>\Packages\Gxpm\Tools\BPDeployer; also, you can download it with the [Business Process Deployer Setup](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?19849). To make an impact analysis, first [create a .bpd file](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?18244) with all the Business Processes you need to update/create.
