# How Can I Define a Search Criteria?

You can define a "search criteria" by:

* Typing a word or phrase in the Search Window (Menu View\Tools Window\Search), or even in the Search textbox in the men ubar.
* Selecting a Property in the Advanced Area of the Search window.

See [Full Text Search](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5277) for further information.
