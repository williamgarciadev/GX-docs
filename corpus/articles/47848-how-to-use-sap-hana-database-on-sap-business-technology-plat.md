# How to use SAP HANA Database on SAP Business Technology Platform

A way to build applications that use the [SAP HANA Database](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?31713) is through the Cloud platform available from SAP, called SAP Business Technology Platform (from now on called SAP BTP).

For trial purposes, a free account may be created at <https://cockpit.hanatrial.ondemand.com/trial/>.

To know how to create a HANA Database instance and use it in GeneXus follow sections 1 and 2 of this documentation: [Deploy to SAP Cloud Foundry - SAP BTP](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?49572)
