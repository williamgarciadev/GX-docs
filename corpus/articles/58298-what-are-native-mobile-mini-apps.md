# What are Native Mobile Mini Apps?

A Native Mobile [Mini App](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/com.gxwiki.wiki?50900,,) is a mobile application developed with GeneXus.

Unlike traditional Native Mobile applications, Native Mobile Mini Apps are typically smaller in size and provide services in a faster and more direct way.

### [Native Mobile Mini Apps features](#Native+Mobile+Mini+Apps+features)

**Optimized performance:** Native Mobile Mini Apps developed with GeneXus using the Mobile Generators for Apple and Android are rendered as native applications, ensuring they fully utilize the capabilities, features, and best practices of each platform.

By being rendered natively through the Super App Render, Native Mobile Mini Apps can harness the device's full potential, resulting in optimized performance. This means faster response times, smoother animations, and a seamless user experience tailored to the specific platform.

**Better integration:** They integrate with the [Super App](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/com.gxwiki.wiki?50900,,), offering a more cohesive user experience.

**Enhanced security:** Native Mobile Mini Apps can take advantage of the platform’s security features, offering better protection against vulnerabilities and ensuring user data privacy. For more information, check [Native Mobile Mini Apps rendering security](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?60365).

**Native Mini Apps are loaded within the Super App:** A Native Mobile Mini App is loaded into a Super App using the native functionalities provided by the GeneXus Super App Render, ensuring a fluid and integrated experience.

While [Web Mini Apps](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?57422) are convenient, Native Mobile Mini Apps are even more robust. It is strongly recommended to lean towards the latter as the preferred solution.

The choice between a Native Mobile Mini App and a Web Mini App will depend on the project's specific requirements and the user's preferences.
