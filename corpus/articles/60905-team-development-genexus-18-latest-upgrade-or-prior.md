# Team Development (GeneXus 18 latest upgrade or prior)

Team Development provides the mechanism for interacting with a [GeneXus Server](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?9911) instance. It is accessed through the [Knowledge Manager Menu](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/com.gxwiki.wiki?5679,,) by selecting the **Team Development**option.
