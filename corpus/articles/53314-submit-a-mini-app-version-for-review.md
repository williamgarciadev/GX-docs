# Submit a Mini App version for review

After [publishing a new version of a Mini App](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?53318), it will remain in **Pending** status. The next step is to submit it for review, where a member of the organization that owns the Super App can finally approve or reject it.

To submit a Mini App for review, you must have a **Provisioning Administrator, Organization Administrator,** or **Mini App Developer** role.

First, click on the Mini Apps option in the Mini App Center. Next, select the Mini App you want to submit for review.

From the **Versions** tab, you will see the available versions. Select the version in **Pending** status.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 56194]`

Once you open a version in **Pending** status, you will see an action button (this button is different depending on your role):

* If you are entering with a **Provisioning Administrator** or an **Organization Administrator** role, click on the **CHANGE STATUS** button and set it as **Waiting For Review**.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 56195]`

* If you are entering with a **Mini App Developer** role, click on the **SUBMIT FOR REVISION** button.

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 56196]`

This action will leave the version in **Waiting For Review** status.
