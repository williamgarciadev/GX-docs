# Huawei In App Public Key property

Huawei Public Key used in Huawei In App Billing platform for verifying in-app billing purchases (in Base64).

### [Scope](#Scope)

**Objects:** [Menu](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?16321), [Panel](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?24829), [Work With](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?15974) (Only [Main Objects](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5770))  
**Generators:** [Android](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?14453)

### [Description](#Description)

Public key provided by the [Huawei Platform](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?47546,,) to collect the credit/debit card information when enabling the [Use Huawei In App Billing property](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?47543).

### [Runtime/Design time](#Runtime%2FDesign+time)

This property applies only at design-time.
