# Attributes and Domains Help and Documentation edition

Every [Attribute](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?7240) in a [Knowledge Base](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?1836) can have its own [Help](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?7785) information and [Documentation](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?6685).

On the other hand, [Domains](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?7221) can have:

* Only read-only Documentation for predefined domains.
* Help and Documentation for domains defined by you.

### [How to open and edit the Help and Documentation](#How+to+open+and+edit+the+Help+and+Documentation)

#### **Attributes**

Select **View > Work With Attributes** in the GeneXus Menu.

The [Work With Attributes Tool window](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?3932) will be opened.

Right click over the desired attribute name and a contextual menu will be displayed offering two ways to open the attribute "Help" and "Documentation" sections:

1) By clicking on the "Open" option (or F12 on your keyboard):

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 51596]`

Note that the Help section will be selected in Edit mode by default:

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 51597]`

2) By clicking on the "Open Part" option and selecting if you want to open the "Help" or "Documentation" attribute section:

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 51594]`

#### **Domains**

Select **View > Domains**.

You will see all the Knowledge Base [Domains](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?7221).

Right-click over the desired domain defined by you and a contextual menu will be displayed.

Click on the "Open" option (or F12 on your keyboard).

Note that the Help section will be selected in Edit mode by default:

`[imagen omitida: wiki id 51595]`
