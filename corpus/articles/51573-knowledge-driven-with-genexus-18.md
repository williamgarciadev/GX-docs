# Knowledge-driven with GeneXus 18

Business requirements evolve, and so do the solutions. Experiences evolve from single-experience to multi-experience, architectures from monolithic to microservices, and the construction from one team to many.

The GeneXus approach to tackle this is knowledge-driven development. Model the knowledge about the business realities, and from the pure knowledge, generate modern solutions today and tomorrow.

GeneXus 18 strengthens this approach by expanding modeling capabilities, knowledge sharing, and the experience of all who are involved in the development process. The outcome is faster development, and that GeneXus is now more future-proof than ever.

## [Modeling](#Modeling)

As already seen in the [Total Experience](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?51570) chapter, GeneXus 18 has new modeling capabilities for the UI like the Design System object or slots in stencils. Furthermore, in the [Cloud-native](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?51572) chapter we learn that NoSQL databases are now modeled as relational ones. Let's focus on another aspect of GeneXus 18: improvements on modules.

[Modules](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?22411) are key for modularization and GeneXus 18 allows to modularize Design systems and their resources: [Design System Object](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?47375), [Image object](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?23387), [File object](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?5852), [Stencil object](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?38418), [User Control object](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?39356). This is key to defining ownership of the artifacts and separation of concerns and is also the basis that allows sharing them.

## [Knowledge Sharing](#Knowledge+Sharing)

GeneXus 18 allows sharing of Design Systems with all the associated resources. This feature allowed GeneXus to share [Unanimo](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?48382) with the whole community and make it the default design system for GeneXus.

[Modules distribution](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?31376)

## [Developer Experience](#Developer+Experience)

We have worked to improve the experience of each one of those who build with GeneXus: from the one who imports a design to the one who models a design, the one who builds a web application, the one who builds a mobile application, the one who tests, the one who deploys, or someone who has just started with GeneXus or someone who has been a GeneXus specialist for years.
