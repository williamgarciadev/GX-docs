# LightCRM with Fiori UI

This sample is the [LightCRM](https://wiki.genexus.com/commwiki/wiki?22592,,) sample using the Fiori Pattern.

It allows sales people to manage their contacts and meetings.

* Manage contacts, companies, and meetings
* Log the information of your lead meetings
* Register contact and company info gathered during discovery calls

It's Omni-channel: Web Responsive, Native Android and iOS applications; all with Fiori look & feel.

You can download this sample from GeneXus for SAP by going to the Start Page->Samples and selecting it.
